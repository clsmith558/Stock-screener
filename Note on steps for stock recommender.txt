### 1. Ephemeral Consensus & Estimate Revisions (Overwritten Monthly)
Why a stock is at 5x P/E matters: if forward EPS estimates are falling 10% every month, a 5x P/E is actually a 15x P/E in disguise. The highest-alpha deep-value entry is when price is still at 52-week lows **while negative analyst revisions flatten out or inflect upward**.
* **What to capture in [`monthly_stock_snapshots`](file:///C:/Grok/Stock%20screener/scoring/historical_snapshots.py#L60-L90) right now (free via `yfinance` `info` / `eps_trend`):**
  * `forward_eps_current_yr` & `forward_eps_next_yr` (tracks month-over-month estimate drift $\Delta \text{EPS}_{1\text{m}}, \Delta \text{EPS}_{3\text{m}}$)
  * `analyst_count` & `recommendation_mean` (Templeton "neglect / maximum pessimism" gauge—0 to 2 analysts or consensus "Sell/Hold" outperforms crowded "Strong Buys" in deep value)
  * `target_low_price` & `target_mean_price` (measures price vs. even the most bearish street target)

### 2. Short Interest & Positioning Dynamics (Burry Squeeze & Trap Filter)
`yfinance` exposes current short interest, but does not give you a clean multi-year historical series once the month rolls over.
* **What to snapshot monthly:**
  * `short_pct_float` and `short_ratio` (Days to Cover)
  * `shares_short_mom_change_pct` (Short covering beginning while price is near 52w lows is a classic breakout precursor; conversely, surging short interest alongside insider selling flags accounting/solvency trouble)

### 3. Forensic Balance-Sheet Survival & True "Owner Earnings"
Deep value portfolios generate 20%+ CAGR not by picking 100% winners, but by **eliminating the 20% of cheap stocks that go to zero** or dilute shareholders at the bottom.
* **True FCF after Stock-Based Compensation (`sbc_adjusted_fcf_yield`)**: In tech/fintech (e.g., `PYPL`, `CRM`), headline FCF ignores heavy SBC dilution. Capturing $\frac{\text{FCF} - \text{SBC}}{\text{Market Cap}}$ prevents overpaying for optical FCF.
* **Net Diluted Share Count Velocity (`shares_out_1y_delta_pct`, `shares_out_3y_delta_pct`)**: Rather than just tracking *announced* or gross buybacks, track actual net change in diluted shares outstanding. A company announcing buybacks while net share count rises is a red flag; a company cannibalizing >5% of its float net per year near 52w lows is a prime compounder.
* **Piotroski F-Score (0–9) & Solvency Components**:
  * `interest_coverage` ($\text{EBIT} / \text{Interest Expense}$) and `net_debt_to_ebitda`
  * `current_ratio_yoy_delta` and `gross_margin_yoy_delta`
  * `sloan_accrual_ratio`: $\frac{\text{Net Income} - \text{Operating Cash Flow}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ (catches aggressive accounting before restatements hit).

Referring to your [`pariah_filter.py`](file:///C:/Grok/Stock%20screener/data/pariah_filter.py) and capital-cycle framework, deep value performs best when capital is fleeing an entire sector.
